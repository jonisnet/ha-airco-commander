# Changelog

## 0.0.1-beta.3 (prerelease)

- No more jumping on a phone: a long louvre label (e.g. *Midden-midden*) no
  longer widens the layout and resizes the dial.
- Mitsubishi WF-RAC: the dial, − / + and the temperature sheet now stop at the
  range of the current mode (e.g. 16-30° when off, 18-30° heating) instead of
  the wider range the integration advertises, so the unit no longer rejects
  the setpoint. The labels at the ends of the ring show that range.
- Timers are open by default; add `timers` to *Collapsible* to fold them, like
  the other sections.
- − / + sit further inside the ring, clear of the minimum and maximum labels.

## 0.0.1-beta.2 (prerelease)

- Brand icon: an airco unit over a cold and warm split, with a snowflake and a flame.

## 0.0.1-beta.1 (prerelease)

- First version: airco card (mode ring, fan, louvres, 3D AUTO, vacant
  property, two favourites) with a daily turn-off time and a sleep timer that
  survive restarts.
